Dot Product: Geometry Behind Similarity

The dot product combines two vectors into one number that tells us how strongly they point in the same direction.

July 10, 2026•Ahamed Learning Notes

The coordinate formula is easy to compute, but the geometric meaning is what makes the dot product useful.

a · b = ∑ᵢ aᵢbᵢ = ‖a‖‖b‖ cos(θ)

Direction and magnitude

A positive dot product means the vectors point generally together. A negative result means they point in opposing directions. A zero result means they are perpendicular.

A concrete calculation

For a = [1, 2] and b = [3, 4], the dot product is 1(3) + 2(4) = 11. 

def dot_product(a, b):
    return sum(x * y for x, y in zip(a, b))

Projection

Projection asks how much of one vector lies along another.

projᵦ(a) = ((a · b) / (b · b)) b
Key idea: the dot product measures alignment while preserving magnitude. Cosine similarity divides out both magnitudes when direction alone matters.
